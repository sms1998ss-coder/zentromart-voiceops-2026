Customer STT: what is the return window for electronics does it matter if i bought it before august first

Agent Reply: Based on ZentroMart's return policy for electronics:

* **For electronics delivered on or after 1 August 2026:** The return window is **7 days**.
* **For electronics delivered before 1 August 2026:** They keep the earlier **10-day window** from version 3 of the policy. 

So yes, the delivery date does matter!
